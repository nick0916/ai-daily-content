## Show HN: Warsaw in voxels – An interactive 3D map of the city centre

把華沙市中心做成可互動的 3D 體素（voxel）地圖，能換時間、天氣並操控行人。

**How** 結合 OpenStreetMap（建築、街道、軌道）、GUGiK/Geoportal 的 LiDAR（建築高度、樹木）與空拍色彩，以及華沙市樹木資料庫（樹種與季節性葉色）。

**What** Show HN 作品 Warsaw in voxels，靈感來自 Minecraft 與 MagicaVoxel；可調整日期、時段、天氣，點擊行人即可控制；桌面版體驗最佳，初次載入較久，程式碼開源於 github.com/stared/warsaw-vibe-voxel。

### 重點
- 資料來源：OpenStreetMap、GUGiK/Geoportal LiDAR、華沙市樹木資料庫
- 可切換日期、時段、天氣，並可控制行人
- 開源：github.com/stared/warsaw-vibe-voxel

Explore central Warsaw in voxels - slightly inspired by Minecraft, and MagicaVoxel. 
You can change the date, time of day and weather, explore the city, or click on a pedestrian to control them (here a bit of inspiration from Dungeon Keeper). Works best on desktop, but I tried to make it usable on mobile too. The initial load can take a while. It's based on several open datasets, including: - OpenStreetMap ( https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=6/52.02/19.14 ) - buildings, streets, sidewalks, tram tracks, etc - GUGiK / Geoportal ( https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/ ) - LiDAR for building heights and trees, aerial imagery for colours - Warsaw's city tree database ( https://mapa.um.warszawa.pl/ ) - individual tree species, used for seasonal foliage Open-source ( https://github.com/stared/warsaw-vibe-voxel ), so feel invited to (re)use it as you wish!
