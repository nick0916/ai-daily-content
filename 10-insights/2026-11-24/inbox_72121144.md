## Article: Engineering Around Non-Determinism: Evals, Grounding, and Release Gates for AI-Drafted Suspicious Reports

把 AI 的不確定輸出當成工程問題，用評測與發布門檻擋住幻覺。

**Why** 高風險場景（如可疑交易報告）不能容忍 LLM 每次輸出不同或捏造內容，非確定性必須被工程化管控。

**How** 以知識圖譜做 grounding，搭配雙層 eval judge、CI/CD 發布門檻，並用 golden dataset 做回歸測試。

**What** InfoQ 文章（Ritesh Modi、Sasidhar Gudala）提出一套架構藍圖，目標是在自動起草可疑活動報告的同時維持零幻覺合規；原文摘要未提供具體數字或實測結果。

### 重點
- 用知識圖譜做 grounding，約束 agent 起草內容的事實來源
- 雙層 eval judge 搭配 CI/CD release gate，未通過評測不得發布
- 以 golden dataset 做回歸測試，確保改動後品質不退化

Moving GenAI into high-stakes production requires treating non-determinism as an engineering problem. This architectural blueprint shows engineering leaders and architects how to build trustworthy agentic workflows using knowledge graphs, dual-layer eval judges, CI/CD release gates, and regression testing against golden datasets to automate drafting while maintaining zero-hallucination compliance. By Ritesh Modi, Sasidhar Gudala
