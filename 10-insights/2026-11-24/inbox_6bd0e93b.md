## Episode 860 | Is the MVP Dead? Steve Blank on What Comes Next

AI 讓人能先做出成品，Steve Blank 因此主張用「初始未測試產品」取代 MVP，先找客戶驗證。

**Why** AI 讓團隊在訪談客戶前就能做出完整產品，MVP 退化成投影片式展示，建得快不代表學得快。

**How** 在 Stanford Lean LaunchPad 課程中，要求團隊在 demo day 前取得 design partners 與客戶；並提醒合成使用者（synthetic users）訪談會產生幻覺，應改做面對面訪談。

**What** 這是 Startups for the Rest of Us 第 860 集，Rob Walling 與 Steve Blank 對談；Steve 表示班上每組第一天都帶著 AI 做好的完整產品來，並主張從 product-market fit 轉向 product outcome fit，以 jobs to be done 衡量結果。

### 重點
- Steve Blank 以「初始未測試產品」（IUP）取代 MVP；AI 的 vibe coding 讓 MVP 變成投影片
- Stanford Lean LaunchPad 班上，每組第一天都帶著 AI 做好的完整產品來
- 合成使用者訪談會產生幻覺，建議改做面對面訪談；並從 product-market fit 轉向 product outcome fit

Is it even worth building a MVP when AI can build a finished product before you've talked to a single customer? 

 In this episode, Rob Walling sits down with startup legend Steve Blank, creator of Customer Development, for a conversation about why he’s retired the MVP and replaced it with the “initial untested product.” 

 Steve also shares what happened when every team in his Stanford class showed up on day one with a finished AI-built product, the first question to ask customers in the AI era, where synthetic interviews hallucinate, and why product market fit is giving way to product outcome fit.
