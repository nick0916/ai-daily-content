## Import AI 476: Creativity and RSI; worse-than-China open weight models; AI goes to space

本期 Import AI 整理幾個新基準與新模型：AI 的視覺直覺推理仍遠遜於人類，但研究品味能力正快速倍增。 **Why** 作者想追蹤 AI 的創造力與研究品味，因為這是遞迴自我改進（RSI）速度的關鍵變數。 **How** TasteVal 讓模型在 8 個前沿 AI 開發任務中設計實驗並詮釋結果，與 24 位專家的人類基準比較。 **What** Humanity's Sixth Sense（Elorian 與 Scale AI）含 522 題，人類 pass@1 為 93.1，最佳 AI GPT 6 Astra 為 53.6，GLM-5.3-flash 26.8、Kimi K3 25.5。TasteVal 顯示研究品味自 2025 年 12 月起每 3.0 個月翻倍，Opus 5.5 達人類基準 2.30 倍（成本約 1/30），作者因此將完整 RSI 的預估由 2028 年提前到 2027 年。另有 Reflection 的 Beam（501B MoE、23B active，DeepSWE 1.1 為 44.4 對 GLM-5.3 的 61）與 Mistral Large 4（DeepSWE 1.1 為 62，對 Kimi K3 的 68），兩者都計畫本月釋出權重，但仍落後中國開放權重模型。內文在摘錄處被截斷。

### 重點
- TasteVal：研究品味每 3.0 個月翻倍（95% CI 1.7-5.0 個月），Opus 5.5 為專家基準 2.30 倍，每次嘗試成本約 1/30；作者將完整 RSI 預估由 2028 提前至 2027
- Humanity's Sixth Sense：人類 pass@1 93.1，GPT 6 Astra 53.6；最常見錯誤是漏看決定性細節（21%）與誤認物件或角色（20%）

Subscribe now New benchmark shows how AI models lack intuitive visual reasoning: &#8230;Humanity&#8217;s Sixth Sense highlights some shortcomings of current AI systems&#8230; AI startups Elorian and Scale AI have built and released Humanity&#8217;s Sixth Sense, a benchmark that &#8220;measures how a model interprets and reasons through information a visual scene implies but doesn&#8217;t explicitly show.&#8221; Some examples of the types of task in HSS are a picture of a bookcase and being asked whether it can fit more books, or a photo of horses pulling a plough and a question of whether the chains bear the plough&#8217;s drag weight. HSS contains 522 questions that cover 288 images and 234 short video clips (totalling 17.6 hours of video). The questions fall into four categories: Temporal &amp; causal dynamics: &#8220;What happened before the scene, and what happens next.&#8221; Physical &amp; spatial logic: &#8220;What fits, what&#8217;s in reach, and what someone else can see from where they are standing.&#8221; Social understanding : &#8220;Intent, attention, belonging.&#8221; Abstract &amp; contextual inference : &#8220;The patterns and rules a scene implies.&#8221; Results: The human baseline for this is not perfect, but is very high, with people scoring aggregate pass@1 of 93.1, broken down across four categories: Image (94.1), video (91.9), time (97), and space (94.9). By comparison, the best scoring AI system, GPT 6 Astra, gets pass@1 of 53.6, across image (55.6), video (51.1), time (58.2), and space (53.6). Interestingly, Chinese open weight models do poorly here, with GLM-5.3-flash getting a pass@1 of 26.8, and Kimi K3 getting pass@1 of 25.5. &#8220;The two most common single mistakes were (1) missing a detail that decides the answer (21% of failures) and (2) misidentifying an object, person, or role (20%),&#8221; they write. Why this matters - HSS tells us whether world models are emergent: One way of looking at HSS is highlighting deficiencies in current systems, but a more interesting way is highlighting how adept today&#8217;s frontier LLMs are at doing tasks which many people have long assumed would require sophisticated, different AI systems to solve. For instance, many people have claimed that true visual reasoning will rely on developing spatially aware 2D and 3D world models, then using that for reasoning. The fact LLMs like GPT and Claude do as well as they do on HSS is something of a counter to this argument. Read more : Introducing Humanity&#8217;s Sixth Sense (HSS) (Elorian) . But could this also introduce a new danger - of AI systems both speeding our buildout of space, and eventually a nation (or perhaps an AI) being able to use space to concentrate power? That&#8217;s the gist of a fascinating article in AI Frontiers. How AI unlocks space: Spacecraft can operate autonomously. Is there a compounding advantage to first movers? If an AI civilization built up a foothold in space it might experience compounding advantages that make it economically and strategically dominant. For instance: Space might support an industrial flywheel where initial infrastructure begets more energy and manufacturing capacity at lower costs which begets more infrastructure, etc. Space has great domain awareness, where it may be possible for a space-based power to deny access to space for others. First-movers could send out self-replicating probes that expand into deep space. Why this matters: &#8220;Space is not a brand-new domain, but AI may be about to drastically accelerate its industrialization, demanding make-or-break decisions of leaders under ever-greater urgency and uncertainty,&#8221; AI Frontiers writes. Results: &#8220;The experimental research taste of frontier models has doubled every 3.0 months since December 2025 (95% CI 1.7-5.0 months).
