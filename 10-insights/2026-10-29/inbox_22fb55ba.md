## Show HN: Point your camera at a landscape to learn about its geology

把地質圖用擴增實境疊在鏡頭畫面上，讓人對著風景就能看出腳下是什麼岩石。

**Why** 作者從他另一個 app Flyover Country（講解飛機窗外景色）的 AR 實驗延伸而來，想讓一般人也能讀懂地景的地質。

**What** Show HN 作品 outcrop.rocks：可辨識圖中標示的岩石、閱讀描述，並把大陸位置倒轉，看岩石形成時的地球樣貌。Android 版已上架，iOS 版還在 Apple 審核、先以 TestFlight 提供；作者在明尼蘇達州，只在密西西比河峽谷測試過，請地形起伏更大的地區使用者協助測試。

### 重點
- 以 AR 在鏡頭畫面上疊加地質圖，可查看岩石種類與描述
- 可倒轉大陸位置，檢視岩石形成當時的地球樣貌
- Android 已上架，iOS 在審核中，目前以 TestFlight 提供；作者只在明尼蘇達的密西西比河峽谷測試過

I've been working on a fun little app that paints geologic maps over landscapes using augmented reality. You can use it to identify what the mapped rocks are, read descriptions about them, and view the earth as it looked when they formed by turning back the continent positions. It spun out of some augmented reality work I was doing on my other side project app - Flyover Country, which tells you about the view from the airplane window. I'm in Minnesota, so i haven't had a chance to test it on a huge amount of terrain (the best I've found is the Mississippi River gorge in the twin cities... I need a trip up north), so anyone that's somewhere with some more bumps: please help!). It's stuck in the Apple review queue, but it's up on testflight for anyone interested. Google approved it quickly and it's fully out on android (links to both on the marketing site). edit: got a report that testflight was asking for an install code. Seems like a testflight app issue, but dismissing it and installing as normal seems to work!
