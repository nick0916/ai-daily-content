## ChatGPT Should Have Killed His AI SaaS. Instead, He's Nearing $10M

Ask-AI 轉型為 Mosaic AI，專攻技術支援並以可量化的成本節省來銷售。

**Why** 原文提到 ChatGPT 讓原產品在約 100 萬美元 ARR 時就近乎淪為商品，因此需要更窄的市場。

**How** 鎖定大型競爭者難服務的技術支援領域，並以 5 天試點與「每位工程師每日處理工單數」指標說服企業買方。

**What** 公司 ARR 達多個七位數（接近八位數），約 100 家企業客戶，創辦人 Alon Talmor 以 $6.5M seed 募資。

### 重點
- 賣點是 50-70% 的成本節省，而非功能清單
- 「每位工程師每日處理工單數」通過買方審查，「解決時間」指標則未通過；一名客戶在試點一週內量到 80% 提升
- 先聽取企業六個月的需求再找投資人；共同創辦人在募資中離開時，立即向投資人揭露

ChatGPT turned his product into a commodity at close to $1M ARR. Alon Talmor rebuilt Ask-AI into Mosaic AI, an AI platform for technical support, by narrowing into a market rivals called too hard and selling measurable cost reduction instead of features. The company is now at multiple seven figures in ARR, approaching eight. 

 Alon breaks down how six months of listening to enterprises produced the product idea, how he closed a $6.5M seed after his co-founder quit in the middle of the round, and how 5-day pilots built on technical support automation turn skeptical enterprise buyers into six-figure customers. 

 Plus: why he believes the next phase of enterprise AI is agents orchestrating people, not people orchestrating agents. 

 Alon sold his first startup, BlueTail, to Salesforce, went back for a PhD in AI and question answering, and founded Ask-AI in 2021, a full year before ChatGPT. Today Mosaic AI serves about 100 enterprise customers, with go-to-market in Toronto and R&D in Tel Aviv. 

 🔑 Key Lessons 
 
 Find a blue pond, not a blue ocean: When funded giants crowd your market, narrow into a segment they cannot serve well. Mosaic left enterprise assistants to own technical support and grew past seven figures. 
 Talk to customers six months before investors: Alon pitched no VCs until enterprises had told him their problem. Yotpo's knowledge chaos became the product, and the seed closed on that proof. 
 Tell investors bad news before they find it: His co-founder quit mid-raise; he disclosed it immediately and still closed $6.5M. Prettifying reality reads as mistrust, and investors sense it every time. 
 Sell the cost reduction, not the feature list: Support is a cost center with a known price tag. Mosaic pitches 50-70% savings on a seven-figure budget instead of arguing about features. 
 Pick the metric your buyer cannot argue with: Tickets per engineer per day survived scrutiny where time-to-resolution failed. One customer measured an 80% lift in a week of the pilot. 
 Treat build vs buy as your real competitor: With AI coding cheap, enterprises default to building. Win by showing the end state their side project becomes obsolete against, not a feature comparison.
