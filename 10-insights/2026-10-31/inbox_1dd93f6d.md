## Tell HN: Scribd Breached

Scribd 通知用戶遭未授權第三方存取部分資料，涉及 email 與付款相關資訊。

**What** 一位 HN 用戶貼出 10 月 26 日收到的 Scribd, Inc.（含 Slideshare、Everand、Fable）資安事件通知，外洩資料包含 email、信用卡末四碼、國別、發卡機構、到期日與付款紀錄（不含信用卡完整資料）。公司表示無濫用證據，已封鎖該系統、通報執法與資料保護機關，並請用戶提防釣魚信。

### 重點
- 受影響品牌：Scribd、Slideshare、Everand、Fable
- 可能被存取的資料：email、信用卡末四碼、國別、發卡機構、到期日、付款紀錄（不含信用卡細節）
- 公司稱無濫用證據，已封鎖受影響系統、通報執法與資料保護機關，並與第三方資安公司合作強化存取控制與監控

Not seeing anything posted about this anywhere, received on Monday, 26th of Oct 2026: Subject: Notice of Security Incident This notice is being sent on behalf of Scribd, Inc., the parent company of Scribd, Slideshare, Everand, and Fable, by Epiq, Scribd Inc.’s notification partner. Hello, We are writing to let you know that Scribd, Inc. recently experienced a security incident that may have affected your data. We detected that an unauthorized third party may have gained access to certain user data in one of our systems. After investigating this activity, we found that the third party may have accessed your email address and the following of your information: last 4 digits of your credit card and country, credit card issuer, credit card expiry date, payment history (not including credit card details). We have no evidence that this data has been misused, but we are notifying you out of an abundance of caution so that you can take steps to protect your information. Watch for phishing We encourage you to stay vigilant for phishing attempts by bad actors pretending to be Scribd, Inc. or Scribd, Slideshare, Everand or Fable. Remember that Scribd, Inc. will never ask you for your username and password or other sensitive information via email or text. If you did receive a message like that: Don't click on any links in it. Instead type scribd.com, slideshare.net, everand.com, or fable.co directly into your browser.
 Call us at: +1-877-361-9887. Hours of operation are Monday-Friday, 11:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m., Central European Time.
 Report it through Scribd, Inc.'s Help Center.
 
For more information We regret that this incident occurred and any concern it may cause you. If you have additional questions, contact the dedicated call center at +1-877-361-9887. Hours of operation are Monday-Friday, 11:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Central European Time. The National Cyber Security Center has helpful guidance on how to recognize and avoid phishing scams here. What Scribd, Inc. is doing We have secured the affected system and ended the unauthorized access.
 We have notified law enforcement as well as the relevant data protection authorities.
 We are working with an expert third party cybersecurity firm to strengthen our access controls and monitoring.
