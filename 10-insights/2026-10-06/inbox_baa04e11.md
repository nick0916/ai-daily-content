## Your ChatGPT history is worth saving — here’s how to back it up

把 ChatGPT 對話匯出成 ZIP 備份，避免重要紀錄只存在單一帳號裡。 **Why** 長期對話會累積成工作成果，若只存在帳號內，取得與保管都受限。 **How** 依序進入 Settings > Data controls > Export data > Export，約 7 天內會收到下載連結，連結 24 小時後失效。 **What** 個人 Free、Go、Plus、Pro 帳號可自助匯出；Business 與 Enterprise 不提供此選項。封存對話不等於備份，刪除後無法復原。

### 重點
- 路徑：Settings > Data controls > Export data > Export，匯出最多約 7 天，下載連結 24 小時失效
- 支援個人 Free、Go、Plus、Pro 帳號；Business 與 Enterprise 不提供自助匯出
- 封存（Archive）不是備份；ZIP 內的 conversations.json 可上傳新對話作為參考，但不會還原舊對話列表、記憶、自訂指令或 GPTs

As a ChatGPT power user , I am constantly going back and referencing past conversations and Projects . Whether it's a meal plan my family actually liked or a resume I spent an evening working on , there are many chats that are just too important to lose. Over time, those exchanges become a history of work you've done, which means, the only copy shouldn't be inside your account. Leaving it there makes it harder to keep that work somewhere you control. You don’t need to be planning an exit from ChatGPT to save your history . The same logic applies to backing up photos or keeping a copy of an important document: if you’d be annoyed to lose it, it’s worth saving. The good news is, ChatGPT has a built-in export option. Here’s how to use it, what to do with the download and where its usefulness ends. How to export your ChatGPT history (Image credit: Future) For a personal account, request a copy through ChatGPT’s settings: Sign in at ChatGPT.com . Open your profile menu , then select Settings . Select Data controls . Under Export data , click Export . Select Confirm export . OpenAI says exports can take up to seven days . When yours is ready, you’ll receive an email or SMS with a download link that expires after 24 hours. Download it quickly and while signed in to the account that requested it. The ZIP includes chat history and other account data. Extract it and check that the conversations you need are there. Settings-based export is available for personal Free, Go, Plus and Pro accounts . Business and Enterprise workspaces don’t offer this self-service option. If you can’t sign in, try OpenAI’s Privacy Portal . Select Make a Privacy Request, I have a consumer ChatGPT account, then Download my data and follow the instructions. Give the backup somewhere to live (Image credit: Getty Images) Downloading the ZIP is only the first step. Move it into a folder you can find again and give it a clear date, such as “ChatGPT backup — October 2026.” Keep the original ZIP intact. If you already back up your computer, put it somewhere covered by that backup. Otherwise, consider keeping a second copy on an external drive or in a private cloud storage folder. Treat the archive as personal information. Think about everything you’ve asked ChatGPT over the past year: work questions, family details, financial worries, drafts you weren’t ready to share. A folder containing those conversations deserves the same care as your other confidential documents. For particularly useful exchanges, I’d also save a readable copy separately. Copy the relevant text into a document, add a descriptive title and file it with the project it belongs to. “Kitchen renovation budget” will be easier to recognize six months from now than another vaguely named chat. Archiving a chat isn’t backing it up As helpful as ChatGPT’s Archive option is for clearing the sideebar, it's worth noting that the conversation remains inside your account. Meaning, you haven’t created an independent copy. Deleting is different because once you hit delete, those chats cannot be recovered, even through ChatGPT or its support team. If you’re planning a cleanup, save anything important before you start deleting conversations. OpenAI does offer a workaround for referencing conversations in another personal account: extract the ZIP, find conversations.json and upload it into a new chat. Larger exports may contain numbered conversation JSON files instead. That lets ChatGPT use the uploaded material as reference within that conversation. It won’t recreate each old chat in the sidebar, merge your accounts or transfer memories, custom instructions, GPTs or subscriptions though. Bottom line: Make it a habit If you frequently use ChatGPT, it's worth starting a habit of saving your conversations. A backup only covers what you saved at the time, so if you use ChatGPT regularly, choose a schedule that fits how much work you’d hate to repeat. Do you regularly save your chats?
