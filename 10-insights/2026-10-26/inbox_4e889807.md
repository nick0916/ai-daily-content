## The Third Product-Market Fit in AI

資安將成為 AI 繼程式開發、客服之後的第三個產品市場契合點，因為攻擊與防禦都在燒 token。

**Why** 攻擊規模創新高：金融業勒索軟體去年增加 30%、今年第一季再增 76%，一次五天的活動就從 2,186 個組織竊取 78,330 筆 secrets；AI 能像攻擊方一樣強力防守，但需要有人付 token 費用。

**How** 作者訪問 75 位企業資安從業者，請他們把非終端的資安預算分配到五類：SOC、SIEM、滲透測試、威脅情資、漏洞管理，每一類都有大量 token 消耗。

**What** Tomasz Tunguz 指資安市場去年為 $175b、年增 13%（Forrester），並引述 OpenAI 的 Greg Brockman：「資安支出應遠超軟體支出」。作者註明樣本為定性為主，圖表誤差範圍大。

### 重點
- 資安市場去年 $175b、年增 13%；金融業勒索軟體 2024 年 156 件 → 2025 年 202 件，2026 Q1 單季 65 件（年增 76%）
- 75 位企業資安從業者的預算分五類：SOC、SIEM、滲透測試、威脅情資、漏洞管理，皆為 token 密集；樣本偏定性、誤差大
- StepSecurity：12 個月 56 起供應鏈攻擊，約每三天一起；單次五天活動竊取 78,330 筆 secrets（2,186 個組織）

Coding & customer support were the first two product-market fits in AI. 
 Black October, the industry’s term for the deluge of new security vulnerabilities, is the prelude to the third product-market fit in security. 
 

 Attacks on banks, certificate authorities & core open source libraries have never been greater. 1 Ransomware against financial firms rose 30% last year, & 76% again in the first quarter. 2 Compromised intermediate certificates let an attacker impersonate any website or service. 3 A single five-day campaign stole 78,330 secrets from 2,186 organisations. 4 
 The good news : AI can defend as well as it attacks, provided someone pays for the tokens. 
 Security is already a massive market, $175b last year & growing 13% a year. 5 That was before security entered the token path. 6 
 To see where that money goes, we surveyed 75 security practitioners at the enterprise & asked them how they would divide their security budget excluding endpoint, the security that runs on laptops. 
 They broke their spend down across five categories. 
 Security operations center. The SOC is the team that analyzes the enterprise’s alerts coming from all the sensors across the network. With time to respond plummeting from months to minutes, AI SOC automates the triage because no human team can read every alert at that speed. 7 
 SIEM. Security information & event management. These databases aggregate all the information across the enterprise & empower the analysis for the SOC & others. AI queries these stores in natural language so an analyst answers a question in seconds rather than filing a ticket to write a query. 
 Penetration testing. The use of AI to determine which parts of a system are weak by trying to break in deliberately. Models attempt the intrusion because they can probe every endpoint continuously at a fraction of the cost of a human red team. 
 Threat intelligence. Understanding who is attacking the enterprise. AI correlates attacker chatter across sources because the volume of new campaigns has outrun manual curation. 
 Vulnerability management. The continuous process of finding the enterprise’s software weaknesses across the estate, ranking them by risk & patching the ones that matter. 8 AI ranks which flaws are actually reachable & exploitable, because scanners report far more findings than any team can patch. 
 Every category has significant token consumption. 
 Greg Brockman, OpenAI’s president, puts the consequence plainly. 9 
 
 Security spend should actually vastly outstrip software spend. 
 
 Every company needs AI security in an age when attackers can parallelize their efforts.
