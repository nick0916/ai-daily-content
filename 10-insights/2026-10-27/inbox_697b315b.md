## [AINews] Anthropic files for $2T IPO in 2 weeks: 1400% growth rate, 48% of revenue through cloud partners, 85% margin, $1B op profit in Q2, $134B cash in bank and $10B lifetime burn

Anthropic 公開提交 S-1，數字顯示營收暴增且首度出現營業獲利。

**Why** 這份約 350–400 頁的文件是外界第一次看到 Anthropic 的完整財務與算力承諾，也引發 AI 泡沫與否的討論。

**How** 文件主張靠「Virtual Collaborators」（可帶入組織權限與脈絡、完成多步驟專業工作的 AI）服務 2028 年預估超過 10 億的知識工作者，並以 Claude 協助研發的「closing the loop」提高效率。

**What** 計畫在 Nasdaq 以代號 ANT 上市。H1 2026 營收 $16.3B，其中 Q2 為 $11.6B（YoY +1,368%），Q2 營業利益 $974M，H1 毛利率 85%；48% 營收（約 $7.8B）經 Bedrock、Vertex、Azure Marketplace 流通，2023 年僅 11%。累計募資 $141.1B，持有現金 $112.4B 加短期投資 $19.3B；H1 淨損 $80.7B 主要來自約 $81.1B 非現金金融工具費用。各來源對累計燒錢（$9.7B 與 $9.4B）和現金數字說法不一，衍生數字需謹慎看待。

### 重點
- H1 2026 毛利率 85%（H1 2025 為 12%）；通路費列在銷售、行銷與夥伴費用，不計入銷貨成本，因此不影響這個毛利率數字
- $141.1B 募資中約 $128.6B 為特別股，$11.3B 為 Amazon 與 Google 的可轉債（約 $5B 已轉換）；剩餘 $6.2B 本金以 $2–5/股轉換（Series H 約 $59），公允價值負債 $108.6B，是 GAAP 虧損的主因
- 揭露近期延後了一個新前沿模型的 pre-training，並有直到下個十年的不可取消算力承諾（含最高 $120.5B 的 NVIDIA 算力）；文件中未提及自研晶片

They quickly complete substantive tasks that might otherwise occupy a skilled professional for several hours, a full day, or longer. AI systems are also becoming increasingly capable of performing tasks that were previously too difficult or costly to complete or surfacing original insights and analyses. We refer to these AI systems that work alongside humans to perform complex, multi-step work at professional levels as Virtual Collaborators. Virtual Collaborators carry the access rights, system connections, and context that their organizations grant them , which allows them to function more accurately and reliably. They can navigate and use internal tools and execute workflows that span multiple steps that require sustained reasoning. Virtual Collaborators are AI designed to be embedded where work gets done, and our aim is to continue extending them to knowledge workers globally, a population that is estimated to exceed one billion in 2028.
