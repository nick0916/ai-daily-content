## When the Central Bank of AI Changes Chairs

Nvidia 不只是賣晶片，更是整個 AI 產業的資金來源，執行長換人會讓這套融資體系出現不確定性。

**Why** Jensen Huang 於 11 月 12 日辭任 CEO，而 Nvidia 同時扮演晶片供應商、創投、錨定客戶與租約擔保人，這些交易靠 Huang 的個人信念與關係運作。

**How** Tunguz 用財報數字推演五個影響：資金收緊會拖慢 AI 建設、新創與晶片生態的綁定面臨考驗、開源 AI 失去最大買家、出口管制壓力上升、客製晶片獲得機會。

**What** Colette Kress 出任臨時 CEO，Harvey C. Jones 任董事長。數據包括：兩年內 M&A 約 $42b、股權投資成本約 $61b（229 家新創、13 家上市公司）、租約與產能擔保 $164.5b、應收帳款 $63.1b；Nemotron 開源承諾 $22b；客製 ASIC 占 2026 年 AI 伺服器出貨 28%，其中 Google TPU 占約 65%。這些為 Tunguz 的推論與分析。

Read on tomtunguz.com → For the past four years, Jensen Huang ran the Central Bank of AI. Today he resigned as chief executive of Nvidia. 1 
 What are the implications for the AI economy? 
 

 1. If the Central Bank of AI tightens policy, the AI economy slows. Over the past two years, Nvidia committed $42b to M&A & technology buyouts, 2 invested $61b of cash on a cost basis into equity stakes across 229 startups & 13 public companies, 3 & extended $164.5b in lease guarantees & cloud capacity backstops that require capital reserves alongside $63.1b in customer receivables, where payment terms now stretch up to a full year. 4 5 Under a more financially disciplined regime facing heightened board scrutiny, any tightening of those equity checks, payment terms or lease backstops will slow AI buildouts just as the broader economy slows when the Federal Reserve tightens credit. 
 2. Nvidia fused the startup & chip ecosystems in a way we have never seen. Under Huang, Nvidia acted simultaneously as chip supplier, lead venture investor, anchor cloud customer & lease guarantor across more than 200 startups. Those deals ran on Huang’s personal conviction & relationships. Nvidia now needs a leader who can sustain these founder partnerships without the co-founder who built them. 
 3. Open-source startups lose their primary patron & buyer. Nvidia has been the single largest buyer & financier of American open-source AI companies, committing $22b to its Nemotron stack across Hugging Face, Poolside & Essential AI : no other company has bought open-source teams at scale. 6 Meta, Google & at least eight US startups that have raised $100m or more continue to train open-weight models, 7 & they will now have to carry more of the American open-source mantle themselves. 
 4. Tighter export controls. The episode likely increases pressure in Washington for stricter semiconductor export controls & compliance rules. 
 5. An opening for custom silicon. Custom AI ASICs already account for 28% of AI server shipments & 15% to 20% of accelerator revenue in 2026, led by Google’s TPUs at 65% of the custom silicon market alongside AWS’s Trainium3, Meta’s MTIA & Broadcom’s custom XPUs. 8 Leadership turbulence at a single-source supplier accelerates that shift. 
 The change of leadership at the Central Bank of AI is a pivotal moment for the industry. It is hard to overstate how central Nvidia has become to startup financing, M&A exits & policy across the fastest-growing segment of the US economy. A sudden transition at the top introduces real uncertainty into the market & especially in startups.

Days sales outstanding (DSO) stretched from a 47.8-day 10-year historical average to 60 to 65 days as Note 7 disclosed extended payment terms ranging from 90 days up to one full year to finance customer data center builds. ↩︎
