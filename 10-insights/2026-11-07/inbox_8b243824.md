**What** 這是一則與 AI 無直接關係的短評，引自 Pratchett 著作《A Slip of the Keyboard》；Simon 以自己為例，提到為辦公室加蓋圖書室，並把錢花在「盲目研究」（沒有方向、純粹出於樂趣的研究）上。

My comment on Terry Pratchett's Study — Hacker News. Terry Pratchett once wrote about the concept of "horizontal wealth". Vertical wealth is where you spend money on rich people stuff - yachts and suchlike. Horizontal wealth is where you spend more money on the things you liked before you got rich: https://chibuikeani.blogspot.com/2017/02/the-price-of-happin... 
 
 But horizontal wealth means not letting your increased income dictate your tastes. You like books and now you have money? Buy more books! Change those catenary bookshelves for good hardwood ones! In my case, build a library extension to your office. And, of course, you buy what will be useful for that most wonderful of pursuits, blind research, which is research without direction for the sheer joy of it. 
 
 (Original source is his book A Slip of the Keyboard - this linked blog post quotes that bit.)
