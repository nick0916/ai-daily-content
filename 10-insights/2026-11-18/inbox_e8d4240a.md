## Show HN: Tinnitus frequency finder and audio notch tool

一個網頁工具，先幫你找出耳鳴的音高，再播放在該頻率挖掉一段的背景音。

**Why** 作者耳鳴約一年半且無法根治，想試試研究中的「陷波音訊」做法。

**How** 先用頻率尋找器做音高比對找出耳鳴頻率，再用網頁 app 播放在該頻率做 notch（陷波）處理的溪流、雨聲、風聲等背景音。

**What** Show HN 作品 Notched（trynotched.com），含頻率尋找器與免費音訊工具；以 PWA 製作，可加到手機主畫面、離線使用，無需帳號、無時間限制。與 AI 無關。

### 重點
- Notched 提供耳鳴頻率尋找器（trynotched.com/find），並附音高比對的方法說明
- 免費 web app 播放溪流、雨聲、風聲等背景音，並在使用者的耳鳴頻率處做 notch
- PWA 形式，可存到手機主畫面、離線可用，無需帳號、無時間限制

I've had tinnitus for about a year and a half. There's no cure, so I'm trying anything that looks promising. One idea people are researching is listening to sound with a notch cut out right at your tinnitus frequency. The frequency finder will help you zero in on your tinnitus pitch. The method write-up is here: https://trynotched.com/how-pitch-matching-works Once you have a pitch, there's a free web app that plays background sounds (stream, rain, wind and so on) with a notch at your frequency: https://trynotched.com/tools . I built it as a PWA so you can save to your phone's home screen and it works offline. No account or time limit.
