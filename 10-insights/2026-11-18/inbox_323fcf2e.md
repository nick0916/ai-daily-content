## Squeezing Neoclouds Pushes NVIDIA Back Toward Its Rivals

NVIDIA 對新興 AI 雲端業者（neocloud）收緊融資支持，反而可能讓自己更依賴想自研晶片的大型雲端商。

**Why** 超大規模雲端商（Google、Amazon、Microsoft、Meta）每季買 $60.1b 的 NVIDIA 硬體，同時在開發自家晶片取代它；neocloud 與前沿實驗室買 $47.5b 且不造競爭晶片，但需要 NVIDIA 的資產負債表來擴張。

**How** 作者的論點：客戶結構多元是 NVIDIA 對抗自研晶片的最佳防線，擠壓 neocloud 等於把議價權交還給超大規模雲端商。

**What** NVIDIA 在 Q3 FY27（2026-11-17 公布）營收 $115.8b（YoY +103%）、資料中心 $107.6b，Q4 指引 $130.0b；超大規模營收 +71%，neocloud 與企業營收 $47.5b、近乎三倍成長，非超大規模占比由 45% 降到 44%。NVIDIA 持有 $102b 雲端與資料中心租賃承諾（其中 $32b 直接與 neocloud）及 $390b 採購承諾；Colette Kress 表示將「有紀律且有選擇性」地提供支持。

### 重點
- Q3 FY27：總營收 $115.8b（+103% YoY）、資料中心 $107.6b；Q4 指引 $130.0b，FY28 成長展望由 70% 上調至 75%
- 超大規模營收 $60.1b（+71% YoY），neocloud 與企業營收 $47.5b（+195% YoY）；非超大規模占比由 45% 降至 44%
- NVIDIA 持有 $102b 租賃承諾（$32b 直接與 neocloud）與 $390b 採購承諾，並要求營運商自備 powered shell 與資本

Read on tomtunguz.com → NVIDIA’s hardest strategic problem is not demand : it is choosing which customer to feed. 1 
 Hyperscalers like Google, Amazon, Microsoft & Meta buy $60.1b of NVIDIA hardware each quarter while racing to replace it with their own custom silicon. Neoclouds & frontier labs buy $47.5b & build no competing chips, yet rely on NVIDIA’s balance sheet to scale. 
 After climbing to a 45% share of Data Center revenue last quarter, non-hyperscaler share ticked down to 44% in Q3 just as NVIDIA signaled tighter financing terms. 2 
 

 Hyperscale revenue grew 71% year over year to $60.1b, while neocloud & enterprise revenue nearly tripled to $47.5b on strong Vera Rubin shipments. 2 
 
 “AI Clouds, Industrial & Enterprise revenue of $47 billion increased 18% sequentially & nearly tripled year-over-year.” 
 
 

 Yet NVIDIA is tightening the bar on neocloud financing. It holds $102b in cloud & data center lease commitments, including $32b directly with neoclouds, alongside $390b in purchase commitments. 2 Interim CEO & CFO Colette Kress laid down a firmer line, demanding operators bring both powered shells & their own capital : 
 
 “We have supported the development of this ecosystem, & we will continue to be disciplined & selective in our support.” 
 
 Because neoclouds lack hyperscaler cash flows, a more selective backstop could moderate growth for smaller, capital-constrained operators. 3 
 A diversified buyer base is NVIDIA’s best defense against hyperscaler custom silicon. Squeezing neoclouds hands that leverage right back to Google, Amazon, Microsoft & Meta.
