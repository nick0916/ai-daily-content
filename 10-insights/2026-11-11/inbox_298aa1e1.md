## Claude Code + Obsidian: My family’s AI brain | Scott Farrar

用一個放 Markdown 檔的資料夾，讓 Claude Code 記住全家的家電、保固、保險與待辦事項。

**Why** 一般 AI 聊天容易偏離主題、忘記脈絡，Scott Farrar 因此把家務知識搬到 Claude Code。

**How** 他把資料整理成 Markdown，用 Obsidian 檢視，並與妻子共用同一個「家庭大腦」，優先任務與近期成果則顯示在牆上顯示器。

**What** Lenny's Podcast 這集由 Claire Vo 訪談數學教育者 Scott Farrar，示範用它比價汽車保險、規劃維修，並使用 DAKboard 牆面顯示器。節目也談到 AI 與數學學習，以及讓孩子教 AI 的「翻轉家教」做法。

### 重點
- 架構：Claude Code + Markdown 資料夾 + Obsidian 檢視，涵蓋家電、保固、保險文件與待辦清單
- 實際應用：比價汽車保險、規劃維修、與妻子共用，並用 DAKboard 牆面顯示優先任務與「recent wins」
- 教育面向：Algebuds 學生家教聊天機器人，讓孩子教 AI，保持孩子自己動腦

Scott Farrar is a math educator and parent who got tired of AI chats that drift and forget. So he built a household brain in Claude Code: a folder of Markdown files, viewed in Obsidian, that knows his appliances, warranties, insurance documents, and everything on his family’s to-do list. In this episode, Scott shows how he uses it to shop for car insurance, plan repairs, share the system with his wife, and put priority tasks and recent wins on a wall display. We also talk about AI and math learning, and why he wants AI to keep kids doing the thinking.
