## Developer Behind the AI-Built Adobe Clones Says They Are “Not Anywhere Close to Ready

有人用 AI 寫出七個 Adobe 軟體的開源替代品，一邊宣傳快達 100% 相容，一邊自己承認離可用還很遠。

**Why** AI 能快速產出大量程式碼，容易讓人高估專案完成度，宣傳與實況落差大。

**How** 開發者用 Claude Opus 5.5 建構七個 Adobe 應用的開源替代品，在 Reddit 宣傳預計一個月內達約 100% parity，之後在 Hacker News 改口說「not anywhere close to ready」。

**What** 依專案自身 roadmap，目前僅約 45% 可供實際工作使用，距離完全 parity 還需 2,300 至 4,100 agent-hours。

### 重點
- 使用 Claude Opus 5.5 打造七個 Adobe 應用的開源替代品
- Reddit 預告約一個月內達 ~100% parity，Hacker News 上改口「not anywhere close to ready」
- 自家 roadmap：約 45% 可實用，距完全 parity 還需 2,300–4,100 agent-hours

A developer used Claude Opus 5.5 to build open-source replacements for seven Adobe applications, promoted them across Reddit with a projection of roughly 100% parity within a month, then told Hacker News the project was not anywhere close to ready. Its own roadmap puts it at 45% ready for real work and 2,300 to 4,100 agent-hours from full parity. By Steef-Jan Wiggers
