## Closing Eight Figure Deals in Two Weeks

AI 基礎設施層與應用層的銷售組織打法不同：前者成交快、金額大但競爭激烈，後者較像上一代 SaaS 但採用更快。

**Why** 客戶成長極快、模型能力約每 45 天更新一次，導致傳統年度規劃跟不上。

**How** 基礎設施層由 FDE、SE 搭配 AE 交付高度複雜的產品；應用層則由 AE 與 CSM 交付，類似前一代軟體但生產力更高。薪酬計畫改為每季調整；團隊太小會缺乏外拓（outbound）與擴張投入。

**What** Tomasz Tunguz 整理 Office Hours 對談：Bardia Shahali 曾帶領 Fireworks AI 營收從約 $3m 成長到約 $400m，現為 Granola 銷售副總。他指出可在兩週內成交八位數美元合約，每位業務可承擔的配額高出數倍，但每家推論供應商都在搶同批客戶。

### 重點
- Fireworks AI 客戶為 AI 原生公司，單筆合約可達數千萬美元、銷售週期很短，但競爭激烈
- 薪酬計畫從年度改為每季設定，因為產品與模型能力變動太快（約每 45 天一次）
- 基礎設施層採 FDE/SE + AE，應用層採 AE + CSM；團隊過小會忽略外拓與擴張

There are two markets in AI : applications &amp; infrastructure. Bardia Shahali has built sales teams both : first at Fireworks AI from roughly $3m to roughly $400m, &amp; now VP of Sales at Granola. 
 In our conversation at Office Hours earlier this week, 1 we talked about the differences in building modern sales organizations in each domain. Here are three takeaways :

 &ldquo;You can close an eight-figure deal in two weeks.&rdquo; 
 
 At Fireworks, whose customers are AI-native companies growing at extraordinary rates, single deals can reach the tens of millions with a very fast sales cycle. The counterpoint is competition. Every inference provider chases the same accounts. The stakes are higher &amp; the competition more intense. 
 
 &ldquo;But each rep, the quota they can carry is just multiples higher.&rdquo;

 Second, annual planning in an environment this dynamic can be a challenge : products evolve faster, model capabilities reach a new zenith every 45 days, 2 &amp; the competitive landscape shifts quickly. 
 
 &ldquo;If you have a small team, then you&rsquo;re not putting in the extra effort to go after, to do a little bit of outbound.&rdquo;

 In reality, they pair to deliver a highly complex product. At the application layer, the AE &amp; CSM (customer success manager) deliver software similar to the previous era, but with higher productivity &amp; typically faster adoption/expansion.
