## My obituary for programming

Simon Willison 引述 Jeremy Scheff 的文字，談 coding agent 讓享受解邏輯謎題的資深程式設計師失去了什麼。

**Why** 程式設計過去是被付錢解「小型邏輯謎題」的工作，而且由工程師自己決定策略、時程與可行範圍，coding agent 改變了這件事。

**What** 引文的核心對比：若你重視打造能運作的系統、不太在意過程中解了什麼問題，LLM 是源源不絕的禮物；若直接解題本身是你樂趣的重要來源，這個損失是真實的。

### 重點
- Scheff 指出程式設計曾是被付錢解邏輯謎題的工作，且工程師掌握策略、時程與可行性定義
- Coding agent 對「重視成果」與「重視解題過程」兩類程式設計師影響截然相反
- Simon 認同這段話是對職涯程式設計師所失去事物的精準描述

My obituary for programming 
Jeremy Scheff poignantly captures the key loss caused by coding agents for career programmers who enjoyed the puzzle of developing code: 
 
 Programming turned into one of the greatest jobs you could imagine. You got paid to work on tricky little logic puzzles. The trickier the puzzle, the more you got paid. But even pretty simple puzzles were worth a lot of money because regular people just couldn't do it! No, they needed us. They would give us a problem and we would solve it the way we wanted. We set the strategy, we made the timeline, we defined what was and was not possible. 
 
 If you're a programmer who thrives on building working systems and doesn't care too deeply about the problems solved along the way, LLMs are the gift that keeps on giving. 
 If directly solving those problems was a big part of your enjoyment, the loss is very real.
