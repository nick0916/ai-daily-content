## SaaS Marketing Without Ads: Word of Mouth to $10M ARR

靠口碑而非付費廣告，Render 四年內做到 $10M ARR。

**Why** 開發者願意替好用的產品口耳相傳，但要綁信用卡的一週試用與沒有免費方案，會擋住許多開發者。

**How** 作者 Anurag Goel 在公司能負擔時才推出免費方案，之後以團隊 seat fee 調整價格，並把讓 LLM 推薦產品、讓 AI agent 能操作產品列為重點。

**What** 註冊數從每月 5,000 增至近 500,000；透過 MCP OAuth 註冊的用戶轉換率為其他管道的 4–5 倍；作者說若只能做一件事，就是讓 LLM 推薦你的產品。

Render spent nothing on paid marketing on its way to $10M ARR. Anurag Goel grew it on word of mouth, even after launching with no free tier, one of the most expensive mistakes he made. Now chatbots and coding agents recommend Render, and signups have gone from 5,000 a month to almost 500,000. 

 Anurag explains what drove Render's word of mouth, why a credit-card trial kept developers away, and how Render raised prices with team seat fees that customers didn't like but paid. He also shares how to get LLMs to recommend your product, and why products that AI agents can operate convert better. 

 Plus: the internal coding agent at SaaStr that picked Render over Vercel, and why Anurag says that if you can do one thing, it should be marketing to LLMs. 

 Anurag was one of the first engineers at Stripe. He built the first version of Render on his own over the 2017 holidays, launched it at TechCrunch Disrupt in 2019 with a team of four, and took it to $10M ARR in roughly four years. 

 🔑 Key Lessons 
 
 Word of mouth needs a product worth telling people about: Render spent $0 on paid marketing on its way to $10M ARR. Developers told other developers because Render was easy to use and kept getting better. 
 Ask what stops people trying your product: Render's weeklong trial needed a credit card, and many developers wouldn't hand one to an unknown company. A free tier removed that hesitation, and signups climbed fast. 
 Design a free tier you can afford: Anurag waited until Render could offer a free tier without draining the bank. It stayed a reasonable share of revenue and shrank as a percentage over time. 
 If you're thinking about raising prices, you probably should: Render added team seat fees to bring margins up to industry levels. Customers said they weren't happy but would pay, and they did. 
 Get five users before anything else: Anurag believes any founder can get five users in their ICP to try a product. If you can't, the problem is the product, so change it first. 
 Market to LLMs first: Anurag tells early-stage founders that getting LLMs to recommend you is the one marketing job that matters. Make your pricing and features pages easy for agents to read. 
 Make your product operable by agents: Render's signups through MCP OAuth convert four to five times better than other channels. An agent that can't operate your product will pick one it can.
