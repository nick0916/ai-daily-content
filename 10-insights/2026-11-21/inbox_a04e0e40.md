## Struggling to find your next great read? These 7 ChatGPT prompts will change how you discover your next favorite book

用七組 ChatGPT 提示詞，依個人口味取得書籍推薦。

**Why** 籠統地要求「推薦幾本書」缺乏個人資訊，推薦品質較差。

**How** 提示詞中填入喜歡的書、作者、厭惡的書、當下心情等資訊，並可要求 ChatGPT 先以訪談建立讀者檔案。

**What** 文章列出 Literary Taste Decoder、Anti-Recommendation、Ultimate Personal Librarian 等七組範本；最後一組要求 ChatGPT 自我檢查最弱的推薦，並標示不確定的書目。

### 重點
- Ultimate Personal Librarian 要求 ChatGPT 一次一問、先訪談 10 題，再分三類推薦
- 要求只推薦真實書籍並標示不確定的出版資訊，再以後續提示自我檢查推薦
- Anti-Recommendation 同時列出喜歡與棄讀的書及原因，以找出該追求與避開的寫作特質

Feel free to refer to the seven prompts listed below and try them out for yourself to see where your next book-filled adventures lead you next: (Image credit: Shutterstock) The Literary Taste Decoder: Act as an expert literary critic and personal book curator. My five favorite books are [book titles and authors]. Analyze what these books reveal about my literary tastes, including themes, writing styles, pacing, character development, and storytelling techniques. Identify the common threads connecting them, then recommend [number] books I'm likely to love. Explain why each recommendation matches my preferences, and avoid obvious bestsellers unless they're exceptionally relevant. The "If You Loved This, Read That" Prompt: I just finished [book title] by [author], and I loved it because of [specific reasons]. Recommend [number] books that capture what made this reading experience special. Don't just recommend books from the same genre. Consider atmosphere, emotional impact, narrative structure, prose, and character relationships. Rank your recommendations by how closely they match what I loved, and tell me what makes each one different. The Hidden Gems Discovery Prompt: Act as a knowledgeable independent bookstore owner who specializes in overlooked literary gems. My favorite genres are [genres], and I especially enjoy [themes, authors, or storytelling styles]. Recommend [number] lesser-known books that deserve more attention. Include overlooked backlist titles, translated fiction, and underappreciated authors where relevant. For each book, explain what makes it special, who would appreciate it, and why it fits my tastes. Prioritize genuinely lesser-known titles over mainstream bestsellers. The Reading Mood Matcher: Help me choose my next book based on my current mood. Right now I'm feeling [mood], and I want a reading experience that makes me feel [desired emotion]. I usually enjoy [genres or authors], but I'm open to something different. Recommend [number] books that fit this emotional atmosphere. Include a mix of comforting, thought-provoking, immersive, and surprising choices where appropriate. Explain the emotional experience each book offers, without revealing major spoilers. (Image credit: Shutterstock) The Literary Comfort Zone Breaker: I usually read [favorite genres], and my favorite authors include [names]. I want to expand my literary horizons without choosing books that feel completely disconnected from my tastes. Recommend [number] books from genres, cultures, time periods, or literary traditions I might not normally explore. For each recommendation, explain how it connects to my existing interests and what new reading experience it offers. Arrange the books from the easiest transition to the most adventurous choice. The Anti-Recommendation Prompt: I want book recommendations based on both my likes and dislikes. Books I loved include [titles], while books I disliked or abandoned include [titles]. For each book I disliked, here's what didn't work for me: [reasons]. Analyze these preferences to identify the writing styles, pacing, themes, character types, and storytelling choices I should seek out or avoid. Recommend [number] books that maximize what I enjoy while minimizing what frustrates me. Explain any potential dealbreakers for each recommendation. The Ultimate Personal Librarian Prompt: Become my personal literary advisor. Before recommending any books, interview me with 10 thoughtful questions about my reading history, favorite authors, preferred genres, literary themes, pacing preferences, emotional interests, and books I've abandoned. Ask the questions one at a time and use my answers to build a detailed reader profile. Then create a personalized list of [number] books divided into three categories: books I'm almost certain to love, books that will broaden my literary horizons, and wild-card recommendations that might surprise me. Include a short explanation of each choice and suggest the best book to start with. Only recommend real books, and flag any publication details you're uncertain about. And as an extra follow-up response, this next prompt can help ChatGPT do another round of evaluation to see which books truly match your tastes and weed out any made-up books it may have suggested: Now challenge your own recommendations. Which [number] books on this list are the weakest matches for my tastes, and why? Replace them with better alternatives. Don't invent titles or authors, and tell me where you're uncertain. Final thoughts Running to ChatGPT to tell it to recommend you a bunch of new and old books worth reading without being as descriptive as possible about your tastes is a waste of time. The chatbot needs every piece of information about your interests across the literary spectrum to tailor its book recommendations to match everything you enjoy (and don’t enjoy as much). Hopefully, by the end of each ChatGPT session that kicks off with any of these prompts, you’ll leave it with one or a bunch of strong suggestions that could keep you and your book club occupied for months on end.
