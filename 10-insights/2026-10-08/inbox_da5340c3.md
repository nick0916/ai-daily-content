And according to OpenAI CEO Sam Altman in an interview with Decoded by POLITICO , "the world should accept some bad things happening" as a result of AI. Maybe that's easy enough to accept as the CEO of one of the world's largest AI companies, but for me, a writer whose livelihood is constantly under threat of being stolen by AI , it's a little harder to shrug off. Yeah, we should probably just accept those so we can shave some time off our workflows each day. What did Sam Altman say? The full quote from Sam Altman is: “I think there’s a lot of daylight.
