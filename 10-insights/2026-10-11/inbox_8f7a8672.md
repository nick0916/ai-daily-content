## [VIDEO] 9 Technologies That Changed Data Engineering

作者分享他嘗試用 AI 把自己的文章轉成影片，並發布第一支影片《9 Technologies That Changed Data Engineering》。

**Why** 他認為短影片比 3,000 字文章更容易吸收，但製作單支影片要花整整 3 天，過去因此中斷。

**How** 看到有人在 X 上討論 Opus 5.5 能做影片後試用：模型不直接生成影片，而是呼叫 Python、JS 函式庫與其他工具逐段產出，從畫面渲染到旁白。內容與洞見皆出自作者自己的文章，聲音用自己的取樣複製，並在發布前多次審查，AI 只負責動態。

**What** 這篇是作者說明影片嘗試的短文，附上第一支影片並邀請讀者到 YouTube 留言回饋。原文未提供影片本身的技術細節。

### 重點
- 製作流程：Opus 5.5 透過 Python／JS 函式庫與其他工具逐段產出，從畫面渲染到旁白，而非由模型直接生成影片
- 品質把關：內容來自作者自己的文章、聲音為自己的複製聲音、發布前人工審查數次，AI 只負責動態

Video is a great way to deliver information. For many people, a short, compact 10-minute video can help them absorb knowledge and insight faster than a 3,000-word article. That&#8217;s why I&#8217;ve tried to turn my writing into videos several times. My ultimate goal is to share data engineering knowledge and insights with as many people as possible. But I stopped. The reason is that creating videos is the most time-consuming task I&#8217;ve ever done. With my full-time job and the time I spend researching and writing for this newsletter, I don&#8217;t have room for videos. One video could easily take me the full 3 days. &#8212; Recently, when scrolling X, I saw people talking about how well Opus 5.5 could create a video. The mechanism is that the model won&#8217;t create the video by itself; it relies on Python and JS libraries and other tools to build each part of the video one by one: from rendering frames to the voice-over. I tried it, and the result is positive. Here is the first video: So I&#8217;m thinking about giving the turn-the-writings-to-videos mission another try; I hope this time, with the help of AI, the process will be more feasible long term. For anyone who is concerned that AI-generated videos are rubbish: I&#8217;m very aware of this, so I make sure all the insight and information in my videos come from my own written article; the voice is also mine (cloned from my voice sample), and I review the videos carefully several times before publishing. AI is only responsible for the motions. So, give it a try and leave a comment on my YouTube video to share your thoughts. If you think the video should reach more people, give it a like and share it with your friend ;)
