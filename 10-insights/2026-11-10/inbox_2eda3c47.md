## Effect 4.0 Ships: Dependency-Free Rewrite with 5x Smaller Bundles and Long-Term Support

TypeScript 錯誤處理與並行框架 Effect 重寫，換來更小的體積與長期支援。

**How** 重寫 fiber runtime，改為無相依套件。

**What** Effect 4.0 於 2026 年 9 月 30 日發布；團隊稱 bundle 從 35.6 kB 降到 7.1 kB，吞吐量約提升 6.4 倍，但這些數字未經獨立驗證。另提供長期支援至 2029 年 9 月。

### 重點
- bundle 大小 35.6 kB → 7.1 kB，吞吐量約 6.4 倍（團隊自報，未獨立驗證）
- 重寫 fiber runtime，改為無相依套件
- 長期支援至 2029 年 9 月

Effect 4.0, a TypeScript framework for typed errors and structured concurrency, was released on 30 September 2026 with a rewritten fiber runtime. The team reports a bundle size drop from 35.6 kB to 7.1 kB and about 6.4x higher throughput, though these figures are not independently verified. It also adds long-term support through September 2029. By Daniel Curtis
