## Episode 858 | How to Build Products People Want

AI 能加快做產品，但不能代替先驗證客戶是否真的要買。

**Why** 作者認為 AI 能加快建造，卻無法取代學習，因此做出沒人要的 SaaS 的風險仍在。

**How** 新書 Idea to Traction 依「從想法到 traction」的流程撰寫，節目強調第二、三次創業者仍會在動手前先驗證。

**What** Rob Walling 出版第六本書《Idea to Traction: Stop Building SaaS Nobody Wants in the Age of AI》，書稿源自 The SaaS Playbook 並改寫三次，獲 Jason Cohen 與 Derek Sivers 推薦；節目也談到 SaaS 未死，以及護城河持續改變。

### 重點
- 作者主張 AI 能加快 building，但不能加快 learning，因此驗證需求仍是第一步
- 書稿原為 The SaaS Playbook 的一部分，作者改寫三次後獨立出版
- 延伸閱讀包括 Ep. 855〈The Eight SaaS Moats that Still Work in the Age of AI〉，節目認為 SaaS 未死、護城河持續變化

If AI can build anything, how do you decide what to build? 

 In this episode, Rob Walling celebrates the launch of his sixth book, Idea to Traction: Stop Building SaaS Nobody Wants in the Age of AI . He shares why he wrote it, how it started as part of The SaaS Playbook , and why he rewrote it three times. Plus, why every second- and third-time founder still validates before they build, and why AI can speed up building but not learning.
