## How a 19-year-old intern built the growth engine behind this $2B AI company | Hector Serrano

AI 語音輸入工具 Wispr Flow 靠付費 UGC 廣告創作者網絡，把營收做大 30 倍。

**Why** 作為新創，在產品尚未有 PMF 時需要可擴張的成長管道；DTC 品牌行之有年的付費 UGC 手法可移植到 SaaS。

**How** 建立約 50 位創作者的名單，依廣告花費抽成付費，每月產出 500 到 600 支付費 UGC 廣告。

**What** Lenny's Newsletter 訪談 Wispr Flow 內容負責人 Hector Serrano（19 歲加入）。過去一年營收成長 30 倍，估值 20 億美元，最佳創作者月收超過 13,000 美元；另談到 YouTube 是 SaaS 被低估的成長槓桿。

### 重點
- 50 位創作者、每月 500–600 支付費 UGC 廣告，按廣告花費抽成
- 最佳創作者月收入超過 13,000 美元
- Wispr Flow 過去一年營收成長 30 倍，估值 20 億美元

Hector Serrano runs one of the most innovative and successful paid growth programs in Silicon Valley. As Head of Content at Wispr Flow, a leading AI voice-to-text tool, he built a roster of 50 creators who drive 500 to 600 paid user-generated content (UGC) ads every month. The creators are paid a cut of ad spend, and the best of them earn over $13,000 a month. It’s a playbook the best DTC brands have used for years, now adapted for SaaS. Hector joined Wispr Flow at 19, before the product had PMF, and over the past year, Wispr Flow has grown revenue 30x and raised at a $2 billion valuation.
