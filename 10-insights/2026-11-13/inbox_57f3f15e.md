Checkpoint Forensics, Chinese Compute and Export Controls GPU fingerprinting claim : Will Depue says initialization patterns in released weights identify the GPU type that produced them ( report ). Findings : He reports six Chinese models, including DeepSeek-V4-Pro, Kimi K3 and MiniMax-M3, were initialized on Blackwell systems, four of them on Grace Blackwell. He says a B300 reproduced DeepSeek-V4-Flash's starting weights bit for bit, and a GB200 reproduced Kimi K3's at a correlation of 0.999995. Validation : He reports a 24-for-24 match on models with publicly disclosed hardware and match strengths of 49–1,385 sigma. He says Kimi K3's weights were created by at least 64 processes, all on Grace Blackwell ( rebuttal ). Scale estimate : He estimates 2–3K Blackwells each for the largest runs. He says senior OpenAI and Anthropic researchers verified the work but are not on record ( request for verifiers ). Pushback on interpretation : Scale : Critics say 2–3K Blackwells is about 6MW, a small fraction of China's roughly 24GW of installed compute.
