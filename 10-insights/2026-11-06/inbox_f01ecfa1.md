## The Ultimate Guide to Product Leadership Interviews

用 1,406 份面試報告與 1,257 筆薪資資料，說明 PM 面試隨職級上升會怎麼變，以及為什麼要在面試中拿到對的職級。

**Why** 職級不能談判，是由面試表現決定；Levels.fyi 資料顯示 Amazon 從 Director 降到 Principal PM，中位數年薪從 $753K 降到 $450K，差 $303K。

**How** 作者整合 160+ 位學員輔導經驗、網路研究、自己 30+ 次 Director/VP 面試，以及與前 CPO Prasad Reddy 的訪談，並把 641 題分成八類比較各職級的占比。

**What** 資料顯示中位數 Senior PM 年薪 $207K、CPO 為 $781K；各級中位數跳升為 +47%、+33%、+68%、+102%。題目分布中 product judgment 占 33%、people 占 21%；product judgment 與 strategy 從 Group PM 的 37% 與 13% 降到 CPO 的 25% 與 9%，change and culture 從 1% 升到 6%。VP/CPO 面試中 35% 含 CEO 面，完整的十大難題範例答案與資料集僅限付費訂閱者。

### 重點
- 中位數年薪：Senior PM $207K、CPO $781K（3.8 倍）；各級跳升 +47% / +33% / +68% / +102%
- 641 題分八類：product judgment 33%、people 21%、strategy 13%、execution 10%；到 CPO 時 change and culture 從 1% 升到 6%
- VP/CPO 面試報告中 35%（16/46）含 CEO 面，Director 僅 2/143；與 CEO 意見不合的題目中位數得分 5/10

What the questions are I sorted all 641 questions into eight categories: Product judgment (33%) covers what you built, killed or would change, like "Tell me about the product or feature you killed." People (21%) covers hiring, firing and growing a team, like "When was the last time you fired someone?" Strategy (13%) covers where the product should go, and why, like "What is your business case for adding AI functionality?" Execution (10%) isn't junior execution. It's focused on shipping across teams, like "How do you prioritize work across a multi-product portfolio?" Stakeholders (9%) covers the CEO, the board and your peers, like "Tell me about a time you disagreed with your CEO." Your story (9%) covers your career and why this job, like "Tell me about yourself." The business (3%) covers the P&L, like "Walk me through the P&L of your last product." Change and culture (2%) covers reorgs, layoffs and the culture you build, like "Tell me about how you have led through a reorg." Where the questions shift by level Those percentages above are blended across all product leadership levels. Here's how the question distributions vary by level: Really, the distribution doesn't shift much. But it's still worth looking at the places it does. Product judgment and strategy contract from 37% and 13% at Group PM to 25% and 9% at CPO. That's because three other things grow: People goes from 19% to 23%. Execution goes from 8% to 12%, because there are a lot of questions around the multi-product portfolio. Change and culture goes from 1% to 6%, because at CPO you're the one running reorgs and layoffs, and setting the culture. So really, you should expect to see more questions like "When was the last time you fired someone?" and "How do you prioritize work across a multi-product portfolio?" Or even "Describe the hardest decision you have executed that affected people's livelihoods." How the format changes Of course, the format also changes. At Group PM , we still see plenty of people getting product cases and PM 101 questions. By Director , that case round thins out. At the VP and CPO stage, we see the biggest variation, so averages tell you the least. Some places might have 5 rounds for VP. Others, like Apollo, where I became a VP, had 11.
