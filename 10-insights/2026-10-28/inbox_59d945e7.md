## Beyond Automation at OpenAI

**Why** 在模型能力每六個月翻倍的情況下，用「效率」框架看 AI 會看錯方向。

**What** Tomasz Tunguz 的 Office Hours 於 11/6 太平洋時間上午 10 點，邀請 OpenAI Chief People Officer、前 Palantir CIO Arvind KC 對談。議題包括管理幅度從 7 人擴大到 100、200、1,000 個 agent，以及 EQ 作為領導者差異化能力。這是活動預告，尚無對談內容。

On Friday, November 6th at 10 AM Pacific / 1 PM Eastern, Office Hours will host Arvind KC , Chief People Officer at OpenAI & former CIO at Palantir, for a conversation on the future of the people function in the AI era. 
 We’ll talk about : 
 
 Why efficiency is the wrong framing for AI when model capabilities double every six months. 
 How the people, teams of companies, can democratize AI & amplify the most valuable capabilities in an organization. 
 Span of control changes where an individual grows from being a manager of 7 people to 100, 200, 1,000 agents. 
 The importance of emotional intelligence, or EQ, as a differentiator of leaders. 
 
 Office Hours is a 15-minute online conversation about a single topic with a luminary in the AI world, where we collect questions ahead of time, accept call-in questions live, in a candid conversation.
