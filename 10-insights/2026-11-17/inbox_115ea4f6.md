## Episode 859 | When to Double Down and When to Change Your Mind

大決定執行後發現不對，就該承認並換回去，多數決定比想像中可逆。

**Why** MicroConf 公開宣布的 Connect 社群平台決策在執行後出現問題，拖延會讓代價變大。

**How** 透過問卷、訪談、流失會員與負面 NPS 確認問題；vibe coding 讓搬回 Slack 變得可行。

**What** MicroConf Connect 原本從 Slack 搬到 Circle（Slack 帳單達 $175K 是搬遷原因之一），之後又搬回 Slack；Rob Walling 與 Tracy Osborn 也談到哪些賭注成功、哪些失敗，以及單向門與雙向門的差異。

### 重點
- Slack 帳單達 $175K 促成搬到 Circle；後續以問卷、訪談、流失會員與負面 NPS 確認平台不適用
- 策略上區分單向門與雙向門，多數決定可逆，改變方向的成本比想像中低
- vibe coding 讓團隊能自行打造所需功能，使搬回 Slack 變得可行

What do you do when a big decision you announced with fanfare just isn't working? 

 In this episode, Rob Walling and Tracy Osborn, General Manager of MicroConf and TinySeed, share the inside story of moving MicroConf Connect from Slack to Circle, and why they moved it back. They talk through the warning signs, the surveys and interviews that confirmed it, and how vibe coding made the move back possible. Plus, the bets that paid off, the ones that didn't, and why more decisions are reversible than you think.
