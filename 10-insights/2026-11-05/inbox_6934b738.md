## ChatGPT can now turn your photos into GIFs — and free users get the upgrade too

ChatGPT 的 Images 2.6 可把文字提示或照片直接做成 GIF，免費用戶也能使用。

**How** 文中示範的提示詞會指定人物特徵保持一致、固定鏡頭與首尾銜接，以產生循環動畫。

**What** Images 2.6 可調整 GIF 速度、逐格編輯並分享，適用 ChatGPT、ChatGPT Work 與 Codex 的桌面、行動與網頁版；文章同時提到生成速度更快。

### 重點
- 可用提示詞或上傳照片生成 GIF，並可調整動畫速度、編輯個別影格
- 免費用戶可用，涵蓋 ChatGPT、ChatGPT Work、Codex 的桌面、行動與網頁版
- 提示詞範例指定「固定鏡頭、保持臉部特徵、首尾銜接」，可直接套用於循環 GIF 製作

It seems OpenAI likes to boost its chatbot’s image-generation capabilities at a rapid pace. Just two months ago, users were introduced to ChatGPT Images 2.5 . However, the most exciting aspect of this new update is being able to turn any prompt you can think of or any photo you upload into a GIF. Alongside that welcome feature is the ability to adjust the animation speed of that GIF, edit its individual frames and share the final result across your social media. You’ll be happy to know that the latest ChatGPT Images update is available to all ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex users across its desktop, mobile, and web apps. While you’re here, check out some of the fun prompts you can try out with ChatGPT Images 2.6 below.
