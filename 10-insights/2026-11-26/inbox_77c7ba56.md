## The New York Times warns photographers not to update Photoshop over AI features

Simon Willison 在 Hacker News 上分享，新聞攝影只做裁切與色調平衡，不做其他修圖。

**What** 這是他對「紐約時報警告攝影師不要因 AI 功能更新 Photoshop」討論串的留言。他說今年稍早有 NYT 攝影師到他家拍照（為一篇關於 Moltbook 的報導），他問能否修掉襯衫上的污漬，攝影師斷然拒絕，表示照片須如實呈現，僅允許裁切與平衡；攝影師還花了不少時間尋找最佳自然光。本文與 AI 的關聯僅限於 Photoshop 的 AI 功能爭議，原文未討論技術細節。

### 重點
- NYT 攝影師的做法：照片以拍攝原貌呈現，只允許裁切與色調平衡，不做其他編修
- Simon 詢問能否修掉襯衫污漬被明確拒絕；報導照是在花園中找無螢幕反光的位置拍攝筆電
- 討論背景是 NYT 警告攝影師不要因 AI 功能而更新 Photoshop，Simon 以親身經驗說明新聞攝影的紀律

My comment on The New York Times warns photographers not to update Photoshop over AI features — Hacker News. I had a NYT photographer take my picture at my home earlier this year (for a story about Moltbook, oddly enough). At one point I asked if they could fix things up if I had a stain on my shirt and they said absolutely not - they present photographs as they were taken, with cropping and balancing applied but no edits beyond that. 
 He did spend a bunch of time trying to find the best possible natural lighting for the shots he wanted. 
 The photo at the top of https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/02/technology/moltbook-ai-so... is my laptop, taken in my garden, after hunting around for the best spot that didn't have too much glare on the screen. You can see how dirty my screen is, too! 
 Photography for a newspaper is photo journalism, and they take it seriously. 
 (Thankfully I did not have a visible stain on my shirt.)
