## Article: Multi-Cloud FinOps: An Engineering Framework for Cross-Cloud Cost Optimization

跨雲架構的隱藏成本主要來自服務放在哪、彼此怎麼傳資料，調整架構比砍價更省錢。

**Why** 多雲帶來彈性，但服務配置與通訊模式會產生隱藏的 egress（對外流量）費用。

**How** 把 Backend FinOps 套用到跨雲架構：用 FOCUS 取得成本可視性、決定 workload 擺放位置、選擇同步模型，並把成本治理整合進 CI/CD。

**What** InfoQ 文章（作者 Vivek Arora）提出多雲 FinOps 工程框架；文中受控實驗顯示，架構調整省下的費用遠高於單靠費率談判。

### 重點
- 以 FOCUS 做跨雲成本可視性，作為優化的起點
- 四大面向：workload 擺放、同步模型、通訊模式、CI/CD 整合的成本治理
- 受控實驗：架構變更節省的費用遠高於僅做費率談判

Multi-cloud flexibility comes with hidden egress costs driven by service placement and communication patterns. This article applies Backend FinOps to cross-cloud architecture, covering cost visibility through FOCUS, workload placement, synchronization models, and CI/CD-integrated cost governance. A controlled experiment shows architecture changes saved far more than rate negotiation alone. By Vivek Arora
