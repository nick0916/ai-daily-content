Bailey picks thoughtful guests and asks probing, surprising questions. His thorough research leads to questions other interviewers wouldn’t think to pose. is one of my favorite contemporary authors. I’ve adopted ideas from each of his books and I make a point of listening to his podcast interviews. In addition to covering Burkeman’s early career writing for The Guardian , they discussed why we shouldn’t obsess over others’ requests for our time and how to make peace with imperfection. Acquired Hosts: Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal Standout episode: Trader Joe’s Each episode of Acquired explores a single company. Episodes are three to four hours long. The hosts say they spend 100+ hours doing deep research on each of the eight episodes they publish each year. The depth and quality of their research, reporting, and storytelling have drawn in more than a million listeners. Sponsors pay $500,000 and up, according to a profile of Acquired in The New York Times . There’s even a Harvard Business School case study about their rise. Their marathon episodes challenge the idea that consumers want only short media. This Trader Joe’s episode lasts three hours and 26 minutes. That’s longer than many shoppers spend in the store all year. Listening to Acquired feels like reading an interesting book. The hosts weave lively details about each company they cover into a conversational narrative. Their sharp analysis helps explain how successful ventures stand out. They explain why Trader Joe’s avoids coupons, ads, and sales; how its founder scouted locations for his first stores and overcame early cash-flow problems; and how the company avoided risky expansions overseas and online.
