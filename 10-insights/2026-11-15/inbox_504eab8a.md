## Migrating off substack now

BiteCode 部落格宣布搬離 Substack，改用自架網站。

**What** new.bitecode.dev 即將改為 www.bitecode.dev 並導向；Substack 內容移至 old.bitecode.dev 並取消索引。作者會順便升級並重啟伺服器，期間網站短暫下線；電子報與 RSS 訂閱預期仍可用，但之後信件只放文章摘要與線上版連結。

### 重點
- new.bitecode.dev 將改為 www.bitecode.dev，Substack 移至 old.bitecode.dev 並取消索引
- 遷移時伺服器重啟，網站會短暫下線
- 之後電子報只含摘要加連結，因為作者認為 Substack 編輯器不利自動化

new.bitecode.dev will soon be www.bitecode.dev , and redirect to it. The Substack access will be moved to old.bitecode.dev and deindexed. 
 

 
 I will take the opportunity to upgrade the server and reboot it, since I don't do it that often, so you will see the site down for a little bit, as I don't plan to make a fallback for such a non-critical service. 
 

 
 Your newsletter and RSS subscriptions should still work out of the box, but I will now only put the summary of the article in the email with a link to the online version, to save me from reformatting the entire thing in the Substack editor. They really are not automation-friendly. 
 

 
 Ping me if something is off on bluesky/mastodon (links in footer) or by email: contact at bitecode.dev
