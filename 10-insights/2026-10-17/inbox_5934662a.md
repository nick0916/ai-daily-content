Hi HN, I’ve been working on esp32sim, an open-source ESP32 emulator that runs in the browser. The fun part of the demo is that there is no ESP32 involved — the firmware is executing in the emulator. The goal is to make ESP32 software easier to experiment with, test and simulate without always needing physical hardware connected. Source:
 https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;mikroverk&#x2F;esp32sim I made a short video showing it in action:
 https:&#x2F;&#x2F;youtu.be&#x2F;waoWFilTWEc I’m particularly interested in feedback from embedded developers: what would an emulator like this need to support before you’d actually use it for development, testing or CI?
