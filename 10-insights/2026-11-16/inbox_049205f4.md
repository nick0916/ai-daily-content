## Strategic Decision or Structural Advantage?

OpenAI 與 Anthropic 各自壟斷不同價位段的企業 token 用量，問題是這是定價策略還是成本結構差異。

**Why** 兩家在 10,000+ 企業客戶中各占約 90% 的不同區段，作者想釐清 OpenAI 的低價是短期補貼還是架構帶來的結構性成本優勢。

**How** 文章引用 Databricks AI Index（截至 11/2 的每週份額，來源為 Databricks 客戶，未必代表整個企業 AI 市場）比較三個層級：SOTA、中階、低階。

**What** Anthropic 在 SOTA 層級的 token 份額從 7 月 81% 升到 11/2 的 94%（Claude Opus 5.5 於 9/21 推出）；OpenAI 在低階層級占 81%，GPT-6 Luna 於 9/22 定價 $0.10 / 1m tokens，7/30 先降價 80%、之後再降 50%，累計降 90%。中階層級 OpenAI 從 7 月 0% 升至 47%；開源權重模型份額從 7% 升到 9 月 33%，Luna 與 Sol 降價當週停滯在 31%。

### 重點
- SOTA 層級：Anthropic 份額 81%（7 月）→ 94%（11/2），Opus 5.5 於 9/21 推出
- 低階層級：OpenAI 份額 81%；Luna 7/30 降價 80%、後續再降 50%，累計 90%，GPT-6 Luna 定價 $0.10 / 1m tokens
- 中階層級 OpenAI 從 0% 升到 47%（Sonnet 5/5.5 vs Sol 6/6.1）；開源權重份額 7%→33%→31%，在 Luna、Sol 降價當週停滯

Read on tomtunguz.com → OpenAI & Anthropic each hold roughly 90% market share of an AI segment, but those segments are different : Anthropic holds dominant share at the premium end (94%), & OpenAI at the low end (81%). 1 
 Across 10,000+ enterprises, that split raises a business question : is this a deliberate pricing strategy to capture different ends of the market, or a structural cost difference in how the two labs serve tokens? 
 

 Anthropic sells a premium product & dominates the SOTA (state-of-the-art) tier. Anthropic’s share of SOTA token volume rose from 81% in July to 94% on November 2, propelled by the September 21 rollout of Claude Opus 5.5. 1 , 2 , 3 
 

 The mid tier is where the two labs now collide directly. Across all Sonnet & Sol versions (Sonnet 5 & 5.5 vs GPT-6 Sol & 6.1 Sol), OpenAI climbed from 0% in July to 47% of mid-tier volume by November 2, & took 57% of the newest Sonnet 5.5 vs Sol 6/6.1 cohort within six weeks of launch. 1 , 4 
 

 OpenAI pursued the opposite strategy, using aggressive price cuts on Luna to capture the high-volume floor. On July 30, OpenAI cut Luna pricing by 80% & then another 50% just a few months later, for a 90% total reduction. 5 , 6 
 On September 22, OpenAI launched GPT-6 Luna at $0.10 per 1m tokens. 6 Its low-end share surged from 30% to 66% after the first cut, peaked at 84%, & finished at 81% on November 2. 1 , 7 
 

 Open-weight token share climbed every week from 7% in July to 33% in September, then stalled at 31% in the same week GPT-6 Luna & Sol cut prices by 50%. 1 , 8 
 That raises the strategic question in foundation models today : is Luna’s pricing a short-term market-share subsidy, or a structural cost advantage in OpenAI’s model architecture?
